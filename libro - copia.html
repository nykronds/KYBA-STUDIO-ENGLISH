📌 Unidad 1 — We are family / A job for life?

🎯 Objetivo comunicativo

Describir relaciones familiares y trayectorias profesionales con precisión léxica y cohesión; dominar los usos de have en contextos léxicos, perfectos y causativos; usar marcadores discursivos para enlazar ideas.

📖 Análisis gramatical profundo

Have tiene tres roles principales: verbo léxico (posesión, experiencias), auxiliar en perfectos (resultado/experiencia) y causativa have + object + past participle. Contraste pragmático y registro.

⚠️ Problemas de uso y matices

  • Diferenciar resultado vs. experiencia en perfectos.
  • Evitar get en contextos formales donde la pasiva es preferible.
  • Entonación y foco en frases con have.

📝 40 Frases modelo organizadas por tiempos gramaticales

📌 Presente simple (have como verbo léxico)

  1. I have two sisters and we meet every Sunday.
  2. She has a strong sense of responsibility.
  3. Do you have time to talk now?
  4. They have a close-knit family.
  5. She has a good command of English.
  6. I have a feeling this will work out.
  7. She has a habit of arriving late.
  8. I have a lot on my plate this week.
  9. He has a strong work ethic.

📌 Present perfect (experiencia / resultado)

  1. I've had that experience before.
  2. They have been friends since childhood.
  3. He has been promoted twice this year.
  4. They have made significant progress.
  5. I've had the flu twice this winter.
  6. She has been working remotely since March.
  7. I have never seen that film.
  8. He has had several different jobs.
  9. We have been invited to their house.
  10. They have been married for ten years.
  11. I've had similar experiences before.

📌 Causativa (have + object + past participle)

  1. He had his car repaired last week.
  2. She had her hair dyed for the wedding.
  3. We had the house painted last month.
  4. He had his phone stolen on the train.
  5. They had their wedding in a small chapel.
  6. We had the documents translated professionally.

📌 Pasado simple (eventos concretos)

  1. We had dinner at my parents' house.
  2. I've had enough of this noise.
  3. I've had enough time to prepare.
  4. He had his doubts but accepted the offer.
  5. She had her doubts about the plan.

📌 Otras expresiones con have (obligación, coloquial)

  1. We have to consider the long-term effects.
  2. Do you have any siblings?
  3. I have a meeting at nine tomorrow.
  4. I have to finish this report by Friday.
🎓 Ejercicio práctico final — Portafolio "Mi familia y mi carrera"
Objetivo: demostrar dominio de los usos de have, collocations de personalidad y ritmo.
Pasos:
  1. Grabar presentación de 3 minutos sobre familia y trayectoria profesional. Transcribir y anotar cada have.
  2. Lista de 20 collocations de personalidad + oraciones originales.
  3. Reescribir la presentación aplicando 5 marcadores discursivos y una inversión.
  4. Entregar portafolio con audio, transcripción anotada y reflexión de 150 palabras.
Criterios: precisión gramatical 30%, collocations 25%, pronunciación/ritmo 20%, cohesión 25%.

⚡ Resumen rápido

Foco usos de have, personalidad, ritmo.

🎙️ Recurso rápido (Pronunciación)
• Ritmo del inglés: acentúa palabras de contenido; reduce las funcionales.
• Practica: I have to go to the doctor → "I hafta go t' th' doctor".
• Sílaba tónica: personality (peRSoNAlity).

📖 Unidad 4 — An open book / The sound of silence

  4. She must have rehearsed for months.
  5. He speculated that the author based the character on real life.
  6. We might infer that the setting is symbolic.
  7. It must be the case that the plot is non-linear.
  8. He could have chosen a different ending.
  9. We can deduce the protagonist's motive from the diary entries.
  10. He must have been influenced by classical literature.
  11. It could be argued that the ending is ambiguous.
  12. She must have studied the role intensively.

🎬 Descripción de obras culturales

  11. She had her voice trained by a vocal coach.
