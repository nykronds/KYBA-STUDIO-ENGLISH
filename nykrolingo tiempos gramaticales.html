⏳ Tiempos Presentes (Present Tenses)

TiempoEstructuraEjemploUso clave
Present SimpleSuj + V1/VsShe works every day.Hechos, rutinas, verdades universales.
Present ContinuousSuj + am/is/are + V-ingThey are playing now.Acciones en progreso ahora, planes futuros.
Present PerfectSuj + have/has + V3I have seen that movie.Experiencias, cambios, acciones pasadas con relevancia actual.
Present Perfect ContinuousSuj + have/has been + V-ingHe has been studying for hours.Énfasis en duración de una acción que empezó en el pasado y continúa.

🕰️ Tiempos Pasados (Past Tenses)

TiempoEstructuraEjemploUso clave
Past SimpleSuj + V2/edWe visited London.Acciones completadas en el pasado.
Past ContinuousSuj + was/were + V-ingI was reading at 8pm.Acciones en progreso en un momento específico del pasado.
Past PerfectSuj + had + V3She had left before I arrived.Acción anterior a otra acción pasada.
Past Perfect ContinuousSuj + had been + V-ingThey had been waiting for an hour.Duración de una acción antes de otra en el pasado.

🔮 Tiempos Futuros (Future Tenses)

TiempoEstructuraEjemploUso clave
Future SimpleSuj + will + V1It will rain tomorrow.Predicciones, promesas, decisiones espontáneas.
Future ContinuousSuj + will be + V-ingThis time tomorrow I will be flying.Acciones en progreso en un momento futuro.
Future PerfectSuj + will have + V3By 2025, I will have graduated.Acciones completadas antes de un tiempo futuro.
Future Perfect ContinuousSuj + will have been + V-ingBy next year, she will have been working here for 5 years.Duración de una acción hasta un momento futuro.

⚖️ Condicionales (Conditionals)

TipoIf clauseMain clauseEjemploUso
ZeroIf + present simplepresent simpleIf you heat ice, it melts.Verdades universales / hechos científicos.
FirstIf + present simplewill + infIf it rains, we will stay home.Situaciones reales o probables en el futuro.
SecondIf + past simplewould + infIf I won the lottery, I would travel.Hipotéticas o improbables en presente/futuro.
ThirdIf + past perfectwould have + V3If you had studied, you would have passed.Irreal en el pasado (no ocurrió).
Mixedpast perfect / past simplewould + inf / would have + V3If I had taken the job, I would be rich now.Conectar pasado irreal con presente hipotético.
